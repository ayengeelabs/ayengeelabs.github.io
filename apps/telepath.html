Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how Telepath protects your thoughts and helps you stay organized.

What makes Telepath different from standard note apps like Google Keep or Notion? ▼

Most note-taking apps force you into a document mental model: you have to pick a title, choose a folder, and format text before capturing an idea. This friction leads many people to simply text themselves on WhatsApp or Slack instead, where notes quickly get buried. Telepath gives you the speed of texting yourself with a familiar chat interface and pairs it with structured note-taking tools: threaded follow-ups, one-tap task conversions, project workspaces, and hardware-backed on-device encryption.

Do I need to create an account? ▼

No. Telepath works 100% offline the moment you open it. You do not need to register, verify an email, or remember a password. You can optionally sign in with your Google Account only if you voluntarily enable cloud backups to your personal Google Drive.

Where are my notes stored? Can Ayengee Labs read them? ▼

All notes, tasks, and tags are stored strictly in an encrypted database on your physical device. We operate no central servers, analytics clouds, or user databases. Your database is secured by 256-bit AES encryption using keys held in your phone's hardware security module (Android Keystore). Ayengee Labs has zero access to your thoughts.

How does Voice Dictation work? Is my audio sent to third-party servers? ▼

No audio is ever recorded to disk, retained, or sent to Ayengee Labs. Telepath connects directly to Android's built-in on-device speech recognizer. Audio is transcribed in real-time across 25+ global languages and accents. As soon as you tap Save, only the text transcript is saved into your encrypted database and the temporary audio stream is destroyed.

How does Google Drive Cloud Backup work? ▼

Telepath authenticates using the restricted drive.file scope. It only has permission to view and manage files that Telepath itself creates inside a dedicated Telepath_Backups folder in your personal Google Drive. Telepath has zero access to your personal photos, files, or other Drive directories.

What happens if I have more than 3 custom project folders and my Pro subscription ends? ▼

None of your notes are ever deleted. You simply select which 3 custom folders remain active. Unselected folders transition into a calm "Locked Archive" state—all notes inside them remain 100% readable and searchable in your main stream.

Can I export my data if I want to switch apps? ▼

Yes. Telepath champions data portability. You can export your entire thought archive at any time to Obsidian-ready Markdown (.md) with YAML frontmatter, structured JSON (.json), or spreadsheet CSV (.csv).

Are there advertisements in Telepath? ▼

Never. We believe your thoughts deserve an ad-free, respectful sanctuary. Telepath contains zero advertisement SDKs and zero behavioral trackers. The app is supported solely through voluntary Pro subscriptions.
