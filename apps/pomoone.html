Manual & Walkthrough

Comprehensive User Guide

Everything you need to master your focus workflows and configure physical sensor safeguards.

1. Access Options: Guest Mode & Passkeys

Pomo-One offers two private ways to start:

  • Guest Mode (Local Access): Tap the dashed silhouette button to enter immediately without signing in. All session logs remain isolated on your device's encrypted storage.
  • Google Credential Manager / Passkeys: Single-tap authentication via Android's native system sheet. Google handles credentials directly—passwords and biometric data are never visible to our code.

2. Clock Faces & Gesture Scrubbing

Switch between three minimalist clock faces at the top of the Clock screen:

Radial Arc Clock

Drag along the Outer Ring to scrub Focus (1–120m); drag along the Inner Ring for Break (1–30m).

Dot Matrix Grid

60 luminous points extinguish sequentially. Drag vertically for Focus; horizontally for Break.

Horizon Vessel

A calm reservoir of time. Drag vertically to adjust Focus; horizontally to adjust Break.

Pro-tip: Tap any interval capsule (15/3, 25/5, 50/10, 90/20) for instant presets, or long-press any capsule to overwrite it with your custom settings.

3. Active Focus Mode & Inactivity Maximization

  • Auto-Maximization: When you stop touching the screen for 4 seconds, all UI banners fade out and the clock expands to fill the entire display as a tranquil desktop beacon.
  • Awakening: Tap anywhere on the screen at any time to smoothly restore navigation controls.
  • Safe Stop: Press and hold the Stop button for 1.5 seconds to prevent accidental cancellation of your hard-earned focus streaks.

4. Hardware Stillness & Alert Tools

  • One-Tap Focus Shield (Shield Icon): Engages Android's Zen Mode, muting notifications, dimming wallpaper, and shifting the display to grayscale.
  • 60 BPM Metronome (Pendulum Icon): Plays a relaxing acoustic woodblock pulse calibrated to Baroque largo tempo for alpha-wave entrainment. Drag up/down on the button to adjust tempo (40–120 BPM).
  • Pickup Alert (Tilted Phone Icon): Calibrates dual-baseline orientation vectors. If the phone is lifted from your desk or stand, the screen flashes crimson with tactile alerts until returned to rest.

5. Visual Analytics & Swiss Poster Sharing

The Activity tab displays your Focus Quality, monthly consistency heatmap calendar, 24-hour peak performance histogram, and chronological event ribbons.

Tap the Share button on any completed session to generate a high-resolution Swiss poster PNG summarizing your unbroken session to share across social apps or save locally.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about hardware enforcement, privacy, battery optimization, and licensing.

Why is there no subscription or paywall in Pomo-One? ▼

Pomo-One is listed as an upfront paid application on Google Play. We believe knowledge workers and students suffer from acute subscription fatigue for utilities that should simply work. Once purchased, every feature and all future updates are permanently unlocked with zero recurring fees or upsells.

Does Pomo-One require an active internet connection? ▼

No. Pomo-One is architected as an offline-first, local-first utility. All timers, sensors, audio synthesis, and database logs operate completely offline. Network access is only ever invoked if you voluntarily open external web links (like support email or legal documentation).

Can I use Pomo-One on a tilted desk stand or charging dock? ▼

Yes. Pomo-One uses a dual-baseline orientation engine. When you start a session with your phone on a tilted stand, the app registers that exact angle as your resting baseline. If you lift the phone, the alert fires; if returned to either your tilted stand or laid flat on your desk, stability is recognized instantly.

Can someone bypass the pickup alert by holding the phone flat in their hand? ▼

No. Unlike primitive timers that check only raw angles, Pomo-One analyzes dynamic linear acceleration and gyroscope variance in real time. Involuntary physiological micro-tremors from human hands (~8–12 Hz) are easily distinguished from stationary surfaces, preventing cheating.

What if I need emergency calls during deep work? ▼

Pomo-One strictly respects your Android system-level Priority DND overrides. You can configure Starred Contacts or Repeat Callers in your device settings (Sound & Vibration → Do Not Disturb → People). Critical calls will still ring through while non-essential notifications remain muted.

Will keeping the screen on drain my phone battery? ▼

Pomo-One is heavily optimized for energy efficiency. In OLED Dark Theme, black pixels are completely powered off, reducing battery drain by up to 60%. Inactivity Maximization dims unnecessary buttons, and canvas graphics run on hardware-accelerated pipelines without redundant redraw cycles.

How is the Focus Integrity Score calculated? ▼

Your score begins at 100% and objectively reflects session discipline: points are deducted for physical phone pickups (-5%), touch bursts (-2%), and premature session stops (-10%). Unbroken sessions receive a pristine 100% Titanium rating.

Are there advertisements or tracking SDKs in Pomo-One? ▼

Never. Pomo-One contains zero advertising SDKs, zero Facebook/Google tracking pixels, zero analytics agents, and zero third-party telemetry. Your focus data is deeply personal and never leaves your device.

Data Deletion & Account Control

In adherence to Google Play data transparency standards, Pomo-One provides immediate and comprehensive data deletion controls:

  • In-App Reset: Open Settings → Reset All Data inside Pomo-One to execute an instant database purge that permanently wipes all session history, metrics, and preferences from your device.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling Pomo-One instructs Android to immediately erase the app's sandboxed storage directory, permanently deleting all local databases.
  • Zero Remote Retention: Because Pomo-One does not transmit or store data on external company servers, no remote logs exist to delete.
