Manual & Walkthrough

Comprehensive User Guide

Everything you need to master your focus workflows, configure clock numerals, and understand session telemetry.

1. Authentication, Google Sign-In & Local-First Access

Pomo-One combines verified Google Play ownership with uncompromising on-device privacy:

  • Google Account Chooser & License Verification: On initial launch, select your Google Account. Pomo-One interfaces with the official Google Play Licensing (LVL) service via secure IPC to verify that the active account owns a valid license.
  • Passkey / Credential Manager: Native fallback via Android's Credential Manager ensures safe, passwordless authentication without exposing credentials to the app.
  • Local-First Device Sovereignty: All focus logs, reflections, and metrics are preserved strictly inside your phone's encrypted SQLite database—no cloud telemetry or company tracking servers required.

2. Clock Faces, Gestures & Independent Numerals

Switch between three minimalist clock faces at the top of the Clock screen:

Radial Arc Clock

Drag along the Outer Ring to scrub Focus (1–120m); drag along the Inner Ring for Break (1–30m).

Dot Matrix Grid

60 luminous points extinguish sequentially. Drag vertically for Focus; horizontally for Break.

Horizon Vessel

A calm reservoir of time. Drag vertically to adjust Focus; horizontally to adjust Break.

Independent Clock Numerals: You can select custom numerals for your clock faces, rhythm presets, edit time sliders, and calendar independently from your interface language (choose from Western Arabic, Eastern Arabic, Devanagari, or East Asian CJK).

Pro-tip: Tap any interval capsule (15/3, 25/5, 50/10, 90/20) for instant presets, or long-press any capsule to overwrite it with your custom settings.

3. Active Focus Mode & Flow Restoration

  • Auto-Maximization: If you do not touch the screen for 4 seconds, all navigation banners fade out and the clock smoothly expands to fill the entire display.
  • Awakening: Tap anywhere on the screen at any time to smoothly restore navigation controls.
  • Flow-Restoration Banner: If you temporarily exit to check setup details, tap the top "Return to Active Focus Mode" banner to jump right back. Because returning is a positive action, it is strictly excluded from touch penalties and never increments your distraction count.
  • Safe Stop: Press and hold the Stop button for 1.5 seconds to prevent accidental cancellation of your hard-earned focus streaks.

4. Focus Protection Tools & Hardware Volume Keys

  • Focus Shield (Shield Icon): Engages Android's Zen Mode, muting notifications, dimming wallpaper, enabling night mode, and shifting the display to grayscale.
  • 60 BPM Metronome (Pendulum Icon): Plays an acoustic woodblock pulse calibrated to Baroque largo tempo for alpha-wave entrainment. Drag up/down on the button to adjust tempo (40–120 BPM).
  • Hardware Volume Keys: Adjust app sound volume in 5% increments directly via your phone's physical volume up/down buttons from any screen without opening Settings.
  • Pickup Alert (Tilted Phone Icon): Arms dual-baseline sensors. If the phone is lifted from your desk or stand, the screen flashes crimson with tactile alerts until returned to rest.

5. Visual Analytics, 5-Tier Scoring & Poster Sharing

  • Monthly Focus Consistency Calendar: Navigable day-by-day heatmap with month/year navigation buttons, quick "Today" snap-back, and a continuous Swiss color gradient bar (0m → 120m+).
  • Peak Focus Hours: 24-column distribution showing peak focus hours throughout the day with date-by-date navigation.
  • Interruption Breakdown: Dropdown selector allowing you to view distractions over Today, 7 Days, 30 Days, or All Time. Universal touch tracking intercepts background taps during active sessions.
  • 5-Tier Score Buckets: Chronological session history categorized into Mint Green (90–100%), Break Cyan (75–89%), Focus Blue (50–74%), Warm Amber (25–49%), and Stop Crimson (0–24%).
  • Swiss Poster Sharing: Tap the Share button on any completed session to generate a high-resolution Swiss poster PNG to share across social apps or save locally.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about hardware enforcement, privacy, battery optimization, and licensing.

Why is there no subscription or paywall in Pomo-One? ▼

Pomo-One is listed as an upfront paid application on Google Play. We believe knowledge workers, developers, and students suffer from acute subscription fatigue for utilities that should simply work. Once purchased, every feature and all future updates are permanently unlocked with zero recurring fees or upsells.

Does Pomo-One require an active internet connection? ▼

No. Pomo-One is architected as an offline-first, local-first utility. All timers, sensors, audio synthesis, and database logs operate completely offline. Network access is only utilized if you choose to launch external web links (such as support email or online legal documentation).

How is authentication and Google account licensing handled? ▼

When signing in on initial launch, Pomo-One uses Android's native Google account chooser so you can select your Google account. It then communicates directly with the Google Play Licensing (LVL) service on your device via secure AIDL IPC to confirm that this account owns a valid purchase of Pomo-One. This ensures unauthorized accounts on a multi-account device cannot bypass licensing, while keeping 100% of your session logs strictly on your device without transmitting telemetry.

Can I use the app in one language and keep the clock in another numeral system? ▼

Yes. Pomo-One purposefully decouples the App Language setting from the Clock Numerals setting. You can use the menus in English, French, or Japanese while having the countdown clock, presets, edit time sliders, and calendar render in native Devanagari, Eastern Arabic, or East Asian CJK numerals. Both settings can be adjusted at any time in Settings.

Can I use Pomo-One on a tilted desk stand or charging dock? ▼

Yes. Pomo-One uses a dual-baseline orientation engine. When you start a session with your phone on a tilted stand, the app registers that exact angle as your resting baseline. If you lift the phone, the alert fires; if returned to either your tilted stand or laid flat on your desk, stability is recognized instantly.

What happens if I pick up my phone during a focus session? ▼

If Pickup Alert is enabled, lifting your phone triggers a full-screen alert and rhythmic haptic pulses until you place the phone back down. In addition, any screen touches will be counted as interruptions toward your Focus Score.

What if I need emergency calls during deep work? ▼

Pomo-One strictly respects your Android system-level Priority DND overrides. You can configure Starred Contacts or Repeat Callers in your device settings (Sound & Vibration → Do Not Disturb → People). Critical calls will still ring through while non-essential notifications remain muted.

Will keeping the screen on drain my phone battery? ▼

Pomo-One is heavily optimized for energy efficiency. In OLED Dark Theme, black pixels are completely powered off, reducing battery drain by up to 60%. Inactivity Maximization dims unnecessary buttons, and canvas graphics run on hardware-accelerated pipelines without redundant redraw cycles.

How is the Focus Score calculated? ▼

Your Focus Score measures how uninterrupted your session was. You start at 100%, and points are deducted if you pick up your phone, touch the screen, or end early. Unbroken sessions with 0 pickups and zero touch interruptions receive a pristine 100% score.

What happens if the app is force-closed or swiped away during a session? ▼

Pomo-One features resilient foreground lifecycle protection. If the app is swiped away from Android's recents menu or terminated by the OS, the foreground service and startup reconciler intercept the event. The session is safely recorded in your history with actual time focused, flagged with an early stop penalty, and tagged as "Stopped early" with an accurate score.

Can I adjust sound volume without opening the Settings screen? ▼

Yes. Pomo-One captures your device's physical volume up and down buttons globally. Pressing either button adjusts the app's sound volume in 5% increments with subtle haptic confirmation from any screen without interrupting your session.

Are there advertisements or tracking SDKs in Pomo-One? ▼

Never. Pomo-One contains zero advertising SDKs, zero Facebook/Google tracking pixels, zero analytics agents, and zero third-party telemetry. Your focus data is deeply personal and never leaves your device.

Data Deletion & Account Control

In adherence to Google Play data transparency standards, Pomo-One provides immediate and comprehensive data deletion controls:

  • In-App Reset: Open Settings → Reset All Data inside Pomo-One to execute an instant database purge that permanently wipes all session history, metrics, and preferences from your device.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling Pomo-One instructs Android to immediately erase the app's sandboxed storage directory, permanently deleting all local databases.
  • Zero Remote Retention: Because Pomo-One does not transmit or store data on external company servers, no remote logs exist to delete.
