FortressFi

Offline-first personal wealth tracking & biometric vault management.

Publisher: Ayengee Labs • Platform: Android • Status: Active Release

Get on Google Play

Key Specifications & Features

Hardware-Backed Keystore Security

All sensitive financial accounts and balances are encrypted using Android Keystore System keys bound to biometric authentication.

100% Offline Ledger

Zero connection to central banking servers or analytics aggregators. You own your financial telemetry completely.

Multi-Currency & Localized Notation

Flexible exchange rate modeling supporting international fiat, localized numbering conventions (Lakhs/Crores), and commodities.

Encrypted Encapsulated Backups

Export password-protected, encrypted SQLite snapshots or sync via user-owned Google Drive sandboxes.
