Privacy Policy for Eduplay

Effective Date: October 2026 • Published by Ayengee Labs

1. Overview & Google Play Families Policy Compliance

Eduplay is an educational mobile learning application designed and maintained by Ayengee Labs. We take learner privacy seriously and fully comply with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and Families Policy Requirements.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Eduplay does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as full names, physical addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or persistent device identifiers from children under 13 years of age (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your territory).

3. Advertising & Analytics

Eduplay does not display behavioral, interest-based, or retargeted advertisements. The app is free of third-party advertising SDKs and tracking pixels. Any educational telemetry or level progress is evaluated and retained locally on the device.

4. On-Device Storage & Permissions

All learning progress, star badges, and skill metrics are stored inside the sandboxed local application database. Eduplay requires zero sensitive runtime permissions (such as camera, microphone, or precise location).

5. Parental Rights & Data Deletion

Parents and guardians have complete control over all app data. Because data is never uploaded to remote servers, uninstalling the app or wiping app storage from device settings instantly and irrevocably removes all learner records.
