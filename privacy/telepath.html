Privacy Policy for Telepath

Effective Date: October 2026 • Published by Ayengee Labs

1. Overview

Telepath is developed by Ayengee Labs as a local-first application. Our architecture is designed from first principles so your personal thoughts, notes, and task lists remain stored locally on your device.

2. Data Collection and Storage

We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. All thoughts, records, and tags are persisted locally in an on-device encrypted database. Ayengee Labs does not operate central tracking servers or database warehouses holding your entries.

3. Google Drive Synchronization

If you choose to enable cloud backup, Telepath utilizes Google Drive REST API to store files exclusively inside your private, hidden Google Drive appDataFolder. Ayengee Labs cannot access, read, or monetize these backup files; your credentials and data remain strictly between your device and Google's servers.

4. Device Permissions

The app may request optional permissions strictly for functional execution:

  • Biometric Authentication: To lock and unlock protected vaults locally via Android BiometricPrompt.
  • Notifications: To deliver scheduled local reminders configured by you.

5. Children's Privacy

Telepath does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. The application does not collect personal identifiers from any user.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated revision date.
