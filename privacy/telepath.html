Privacy Policy for Telepath

Effective Date: October 19, 2026 • Published by Ayengee Labs

1. Introduction & Privacy Philosophy

At Ayengee Labs, we believe that personal notes, voice thoughts, and mind-maps are among the most sensitive information a person generates. Your thoughts deserve an uncompromising sanctuary.

Telepath: Notes to Self is engineered from the ground up as an offline-first, zero-knowledge, hardware-encrypted application.

  • Zero Company Servers: We operate no central servers, web databases, or analytics clouds that ingest your notes.
  • Zero Advertising Trackers: We include no advertising SDKs, behavioral profiling trackers, or third-party marketing beacons.
  • Hardware-Backed Encryption: Your database is encrypted on your physical device using 256-bit AES SQLCipher with keys protected by the Android Keystore.
  • Data Sovereignty: You have complete control to export, back up, or permanently destroy your data at any time.

2. Information We Collect and Process

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device (Encrypted): Note text, follow-up evolutions, task checklists, speech transcription text, tags, project categories, reminder timestamps, and local UI preferences. Ayengee Labs has no technical ability to read, decrypt, or access your database.

B. Device Permissions & Justification:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used exclusively during active speech dictation. Audio is transcribed on-device in real-time. Raw audio is never stored to disk or transmitted to any server.
  • Exact Alarms & Notifications: Used to deliver your configured task and thought reminders.
  • Biometrics: Used strictly via Android BiometricPrompt to unlock protected notes locally. Biometric templates are never visible to the application.
  • Internet Access: Used only for optional Google Drive backups, resolving user-pasted Markdown image links, and Google Play Billing subscription validation.

3. Google Drive Cloud Backup (Optional)

If you voluntarily choose to connect your Google Account for cloud backups, authentication occurs through Google Play Services OAuth using the restricted drive.file scope. Telepath only has access to files it creates inside an isolated Telepath_Backups folder. It cannot read, view, or modify any other files in your personal Google Drive.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

  • 30-Day Recycle Bin: Deleted thoughts and projects are safely kept for 30 days before automatic purge, with instant restore options.
  • Immediate Permanent Deletion: Tapping "Delete Forever" immediately purges records and relational nodes from local disk.
  • Clear Local Data: You can wipe all local databases, settings, and cache files at any time via Settings > Privacy & Security.
  • Cloud Backup Purge: You can delete your cloud backup directly from within Telepath settings or from your Google Drive.

5. Children's Privacy

Telepath is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. Because all data is held locally on your device without central servers, we do not knowingly solicit or maintain personal information from children.

6. Security Practices

We implement SQLCipher 256-bit AES database encryption, Android Keystore hardware-backed keys, and TLS 1.3 transport security for Google Drive API synchronization within Android's sandboxed process boundary.
