Privacy Policy for FortressFi

Effective Date: October 2026 • Published by Ayengee Labs

1. Overview

FortressFi is an offline-first wealth and portfolio vault built by Ayengee Labs. Financial privacy is a fundamental human right; therefore, FortressFi is architected to operate completely offline without transmitting your financial records anywhere.

2. Zero Telemetry & On-Device Storage

We do not track, collect, store, or sell your financial data. All asset accounts, balances, transactions, and notes are encrypted on your local Android device using hardware-backed keystores. Ayengee Labs has no visibility into your portfolio or holdings.

3. Device Permissions & Biometrics

FortressFi requests minimal permissions required exclusively for security and functionality:

  • USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT: Used solely to unlock encrypted local vaults via Android's native BiometricPrompt API. Biometric credentials never leave your hardware security module (TEE/StrongBox) and are never accessible to our application.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

FortressFi contains no third-party tracking SDKs, no advertisement libraries, and no telemetry services. Network calls are only executed if you trigger manual currency exchange rate updates, which are fetched without passing any user or account identifiers.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data lives on your device, deleting the application or clearing app storage from Android settings permanently deletes all encrypted records. We cannot restore your data if you lose your vault password or encryption key.
