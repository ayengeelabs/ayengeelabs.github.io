2. Description of the Service & Architectural Model

Telepath is an offline-first, hardware-encrypted note-taking, voice transcription, and personal task management application.

Local Sovereignty: All thoughts, tasks, hashtags, images, audio transcripts, and relations you input are stored directly on your physical Android device in a 256-bit AES SQLCipher encrypted SQLite database.

Zero Central Custody: Ayengee Labs does not operate central servers that store, mirror, index, or possess custody of your notes or encryption keys.

Optional Cloud Backup: Telepath provides an optional cloud backup integration with the Google Drive REST API. If you choose to enable this feature, your encrypted backup files are uploaded exclusively to your personal Google Drive account in an isolated Telepath_Backups folder.
