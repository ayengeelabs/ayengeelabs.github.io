Telepath: Notes to Self — User Guide

A complete walkthrough of capturing thoughts, branching follow-ups, organizing projects, and preserving privacy.

1. Interface Overview & Navigation

Telepath is built around the effortless feel of a messaging app, reimagined as a calm, personal thought sanctuary:

Notes Stream

Your primary chronological stream. Shows your recent thoughts, follow-ups, to-dos, and quick captures.

Projects & Topics

A visual workspace grid organizing your notes into focused folders (#general, #work, #ideas).

30-Day Recycle Bin

A calm safety buffer. Deleted thoughts are safely staged for 30 days with full one-tap recovery.

2. Fast Capture & Stream Basics

  • Quick Text Capture: Tap the bottom capture dock ("Note to self..."), type your thought, and tap the Send button or press Enter. Your thought instantly enters your stream.
  • Inline Hashtags: Type #tagname anywhere inside your note. Telepath automatically detects the tag and groups it into the matching project folder while keeping it in your main stream.
  • Image & Link Attachments: Tap the photo icon to attach photos or screenshots (with pinch-to-zoom up to 5x), or tap the link icon to attach clean web links.
  • Android Quick Settings Tile: Add the "Telepath Note" tile to your Android notification tray to capture fleeting ideas over any app without opening your stream.

3. Voice Dictation (25+ Languages)

Walking, driving, or thinking out loud? Tap the microphone icon on the capture bar:

  • Real-Time Waveform: Speak naturally while the app transcribes your words in real time.
  • On-Device Privacy: Audio processing is executed locally via Android's built-in speech engine. Raw audio is never saved to disk or transmitted to any server.
  • Global Accents & Languages: Tap the language chip to instantly switch between 25+ languages and regional accents including English (US, UK, India), Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Japanese, and more.

4. Threaded Follow-ups & Idea Trees

In standard messaging apps, adding to an idea days later separates your thoughts with unrelated chatter. In Telepath:

  • Branching Follow-ups: Tap "Follow-up" on any note card to attach sub-notes, iterations, photos, or voice clips directly under the root idea.
  • Clean 2-Level Depth: Ideas are kept focused and clean without infinite, confusing nested sub-folders.
  • Idea Tree View: Tap any thought card to open the visual Idea Tree, displaying all related follow-ups and chronological evolutions in one cohesive view.
  • Promoting Notes: If a sub-thought evolves into a major venture, tap options and choose "Detach to Parent" to promote it into an independent root note.

5. Actionable Tasks & One-Tap Conversions

  • Turn Thoughts into To-Dos: Tap "Make Task" on any note or follow-up to convert it into a checkbox to-do.
  • Track Progress: Tapping the checkbox marks the task complete with tactile haptic feedback, strikethrough styling, and a completion timestamp.
  • Reversible: If an idea expands or was marked by mistake, tap "Undo Task" to restore it to a note without losing context.

6. Projects, Filters & Reviews

  • Stream Filters: Use the "All Notes" menu to filter your stream by specific projects, or filter by Pinned, Tasks, Reminders, or This Week.
  • Exact Reminders: Set exact-minute reminders with customizable recurrence (Daily, Weekdays, Weekly, Monthly for Pro users).
  • Weekly Review: Open the Weekly Review dashboard to reflect on your completed tasks, review dormant thoughts that need attention, and track your creative velocity.

7. Data Sovereignty, Backups & Export

  • 256-bit AES Encryption: All notes live in an encrypted SQLite database using SQLCipher. Master keys are protected by your device's hardware security module (Android Keystore).
  • Google Drive Backup: Connect your personal Google Account to backup your notes to a private Telepath_Backups folder. Zero third-party cloud servers have access.
  • Clean Exports: Export your database anytime to Obsidian-ready Markdown (.md), structured JSON, or spreadsheet CSV.
